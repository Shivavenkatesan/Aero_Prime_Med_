Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for buyers

What products does Aero Prime Med supply?

The proposed categories include medical consumables, hospital supplies, respiratory products, urology, dental, laboratory and home healthcare. Confirm the actual catalogue with the business.

Can I request a bulk quotation?

Yes. Submit product details, quantity, specifications and destination. Connect the demo form to a business inbox or CRM before launch.

Can I enquire from another country?

International enquiries can be submitted, but export feasibility, shipping and local regulatory requirements must be confirmed.

Can I request product documentation?

Yes. Include the product name, model or reference. Available documentation depends on the item and manufacturer.
