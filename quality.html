Product information

Clear information for informed procurement.

Product specifications, manufacturer details and applicable documentation help buyers evaluate medical products responsibly.

Product documentation

Depending on the product, relevant information may include:

  • Product specifications and technical data
  • Manufacturer details and model references
  • Instructions for use, where applicable
  • Applicable certificates and declarations
  • Traceability or batch information, where relevant
  • Packaging, storage and handling information

Product-specific verification matters

Regulatory status and documentation vary by product, model, manufacturer and destination market. A certificate or approval for one item must not be represented as applying to every catalogue item.
